darkwinter Signal

The 3D darkwinter mark with the Mark Lab sounds, packaged as one drop-in element. Move over it and the hypercube turns through the fourth dimension, playing the era's scale. Click or tap to send a signal, which plays the darkwinter sonic logo. Sound stays off until a visitor turns it on.

Two files, one tag

Host darkwinter-signal.js and darkwinter-signal-sound.js side by side on the site. The sound file only downloads when a visitor turns sound on. It works in Webflow, Framer, Next.js or plain HTML.

<script type="module" src="/js/darkwinter-signal.js"></script>

<darkwinter-signal era="01" eras="01,02,03,04,05,06,00"
  style="height: 520px"></darkwinter-signal>
