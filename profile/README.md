## What we believe

- AI adoption should start with one useful workflow, not a vague transformation program.
- The best first use case is practical, mission-aligned, low-risk, and visible enough to build confidence.
- Capacity matters, but trust matters more.
- Tools are not the strategy. The workflow is the strategy.
- The goal is not to move first. The goal is to move with clarity.

## The Four Storms

Most teams are not blocked by lack of interest. They are blocked by one or more storms:

1. **Noise Storm:** Too much information, not enough signal.
2. **Resource Storm:** Big ambitions, constrained capacity.
3. **Change Storm:** Adoption friction, unclear ownership, and team hesitation.
4. **Values Storm:** Trust, data, quality, and mission risks that cannot be ignored.

Our free resources help you identify the storm. Our paid kits and advisory work help you move through it.
