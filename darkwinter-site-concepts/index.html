Two directions for the new site, built at real scale so you can play with them. Both put the campaign line, "Who owns the future?", up front, and both make the mark itself the guide: each part of it leads to one of the four starting points and has its own sound. Turn sound on in either frame, then hover, click and drag the mark. The copy follows the site report. Anything that would need real AI or a backend is marked as scripted.

Concept A

The Signal: the mark is the front door

darkwinter.ai
Transmission · Who owns the future
// Campaign 01

Who owns
the future?

Everyone deserves the power to build it. darkwinter helps founders, growing teams and mission-driven organizations put AI to work, from the first useful workflow to connected systems their people run.

The idea

The mark is the homepage. It's a large, living, 3D instrument in a snowstorm, and its four parts are the four ways to start. Hover a part to hear it and read what it means. Click it, and the sonic logo plays as that path opens beside the mark.

How it guides

  • Core (the red beacon): Get started
  • Ring: Build team capability
  • Frame: Connect and automate
  • Lattice (the hypercube): Your AI operating system

The buttons along the bottom do the same thing, for keyboard, touch and screen readers.

How it sounds

  • Core: the signal bell
  • Ring: a slow chord
  • Frame: a deep structural pulse
  • Lattice: a rising arpeggio, played note by note as you drag it through the fourth dimension
  • Selecting a part plays the sonic logo, A–C–E resolving to F

Below the fold

The rest of the site scrolls normally: the path pages, the resources, the studio, the manifesto. Each one reuses the part's colour and sound as its accent, so the front door teaches you the map of the whole site.

Concept B

The Operator: a guide that talks you to your starting point

Operator // darkwinter.os● Signal open

The idea

A cyberpunk console that asks three plain questions and lights up the parts of the mark that match your answers. It ends with a starting brief: your path, three first steps, a free resource, and a conversation starter that's already filled in.

Where the AI comes in

This mockup is scripted. On the live site, the last step can use Claude to draft your brief from your own words, as a real demonstration of the work. It would be clearly labelled as AI, rate-limited, and would never store what you type without asking.

Meeting people where they are

Someone who has never used AI and a team preparing agents both get a useful answer in under a minute, with no sign-up. The brief is theirs to copy or download.

Combined

The two concepts fit together. Concept A is the front door, and the Operator opens when you press "Find your starting point" or select a part.
